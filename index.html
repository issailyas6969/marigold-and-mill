A small room with a long memory

Marigold & Mill opened in 2016 in a former hardware shop. We kept the tin ceiling, the original counter, and the faint smell of sawdust in the back room that nobody has managed to shift.

Nadia runs the roaster — a 5kg drum she has had since the beginning. She buys green coffee from three farms she visits each year: Finca La Guaca in Huila, a smallholder co-op in Yirgacheffe, and a family plot above Antigua.

Everything else comes from within forty miles. Milk from Wrenfield Dairy, flour from Ostler Mill, eggs from the Saturday market, and whichever grower turned up with too many courgettes.
